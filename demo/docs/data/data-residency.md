# Data residency

Data residency decides the region where your workspace data is stored.

## Regions

Northstar Desk has two regions: the US region in Oregon and the EU region in Frankfurt. Every plan can choose either region.

## What stays in the region

Tickets, comments, contacts, attachments, and backups are stored in the region you chose. Search indexes and exports are built in the same region.

## Choosing a region

You choose the region when you create the workspace. The region cannot be changed later.

## Moving to another region

To move, create a new workspace in the other region and move your data with an export and import. For Enterprise customers, our support team runs the import for you. For other plans, the import uses the API.

## Email routing

Customer emails are received by servers in the same region as your workspace. Emails you send leave from the same region too.
