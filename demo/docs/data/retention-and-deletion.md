# Data retention and deletion

This page explains how long Northstar Desk keeps your data and how to delete it.

## Default retention

Tickets, contacts, and attachments are kept for as long as your subscription is active. There is no automatic deletion on Starter or Team.

## Deleted tickets

A deleted ticket moves to the Trash view. It stays there for 30 days and can be restored by an Admin. After 30 days it is permanently deleted.

## Retention policies on Enterprise

Enterprise workspaces can create retention policies under Settings > Data > Retention. A policy deletes tickets that were closed more than a chosen number of months ago. The shortest allowed period is 3 months. Deletions by a policy skip the Trash and cannot be undone.

## Redact a contact

To answer a privacy request from one of your customers, open the contact and choose Redact. Redaction removes that person's name, email address, and message text from all their tickets. It cannot be undone.

## Delete the whole workspace

The Owner can delete the workspace from Settings > Workspace > Delete workspace. Data is removed from our production systems within 30 days and from backups within 90 days.
