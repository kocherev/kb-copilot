# Free trial

Every new workspace starts with a free trial so you can try Northstar Desk with your real support email.

## What the trial includes

The trial lasts 14 days and includes every feature of the Team plan, including the API, webhooks, and the Slack integration. You can invite up to 10 people during the trial. No credit card is needed to start.

## Extending the trial

The Owner can extend the trial once, by 7 days, from Settings > Billing. The extension must be requested before the trial ends. Our sales team can arrange a longer evaluation for companies looking at Enterprise.

## When the trial ends

If you have not picked a plan when the trial ends, the workspace becomes read-only. Your tickets and settings are kept, and choosing a plan brings everything back. A read-only trial workspace is deleted after 30 days without a plan.

## Features that are not in the trial

Single sign-on, SCIM, and the audit log are Enterprise features and are not part of the standard trial. Ask the sales team for an Enterprise trial if you need to test them.
