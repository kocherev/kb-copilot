# Seats

Each Owner, Admin, and Agent uses one paid seat. Light agents do not use a paid seat.

## Adding seats

The Owner adds seats from Settings > Billing > Seats. New seats are available right away. You pay a pro-rated charge for the days left in the current billing period, and it appears on a separate invoice issued the same day.

## Removing seats

You can lower your seat count at any time, but the change takes effect at your next renewal. Until then the seats stay on your plan and you can keep using them. Removed seats are not refunded.

## Seats and suspended people

When someone is removed from the workspace or suspended through SCIM, their seat becomes free for another person. The seat itself stays on your bill until you lower the seat count.

## Light agents

Light agents are free. The Team plan includes up to 10 Light agents, and Enterprise has no limit. Starter does not include Light agents. If you need an eleventh Light agent on Team, upgrade one of them to Agent or move to Enterprise.

## Seat limits per plan

Starter allows up to 3 paid seats, and Team allows up to 50. Enterprise has no upper seat limit, but contracts start at 20 seats.
