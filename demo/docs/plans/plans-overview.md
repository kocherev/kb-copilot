# Plans and pricing

Northstar Desk has three plans: Starter, Team, and Enterprise. Prices are per paid seat.

## Starter

Starter is for small teams getting started. It costs $19 per seat per month on monthly billing, or $15 per seat per month on annual billing. Starter allows up to 3 paid seats and 1 support email address. It does not include the API, webhooks, or the Slack integration.

## Team

Team is for growing support teams. It costs $49 per seat per month on monthly billing, or $39 per seat per month on annual billing. Team allows up to 50 paid seats, includes up to 10 free Light agents, and adds the API, webhooks, the Slack integration, and SLA policies.

## Enterprise

Enterprise adds single sign-on, SCIM provisioning, the audit log, custom retention policies, and a 99.9% uptime commitment. Pricing is quoted by our sales team. Enterprise is sold on annual contracts only, with a minimum of 20 paid seats.

## Annual billing

Annual billing saves about 20% compared with paying monthly. You can switch from monthly to annual billing at any time from Settings > Billing. Switching from annual to monthly takes effect at the next renewal.

## Changing plans

An upgrade takes effect immediately, and you pay a pro-rated amount for the rest of the current period. A downgrade takes effect at the next renewal. You cannot downgrade if you use more seats or features than the lower plan allows.
