# Quickstart

This page walks a new team from sign-up to a first answered ticket. Most teams finish it in under an hour.

## Five steps to a working help desk

1. Create a workspace and pick a data region.
2. Connect your support email address so customer messages become tickets.
3. Invite your teammates and give each one a role.
4. Save a first macro for an answer you send often.
5. Send a test email to your support address and reply to it from Northstar Desk.

The setup checklist on the home screen tracks these steps. It disappears once all five are done.

## Your free trial

Every new workspace starts a 14-day free trial with all Team plan features. No credit card is needed to start. You can pick a plan at any time from Settings > Billing.

## Where to go next

Read the page on roles before you invite people, because roles decide who can change settings and who can see billing. If your company uses a single sign-on provider, read the SSO overview before you invite a large team. Enterprise workspaces can create users automatically at their first sign-in.

## Getting help

The help button in the bottom left corner opens a chat with the Northstar support team. Support answers in English during business hours in the United States and Europe. Enterprise customers also get a named contact.
