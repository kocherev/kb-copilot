# Create a workspace

A workspace holds your tickets, teammates, channels, and settings. Each company usually needs only one.

## Sign up

Go to northstardesk.example/signup and enter your work email address. Northstar Desk sends a six-digit code to confirm the address. The person who signs up becomes the Owner of the new workspace.

## Choose a name and a slug

The workspace name is shown to your teammates and in emails to customers. The slug is the short name in your web address, such as acme.northstardesk.example. A slug must be 3 to 30 characters long and may use lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens. The Owner can change the slug once every 30 days from Settings > Workspace. Old links stop working after a slug change.

## Pick a data region

During sign-up you choose where your data is stored: the US region in Oregon or the EU region in Frankfurt. The region cannot be changed after the workspace is created, so check with your security team first. The data residency page explains what the region covers.

## After sign-up

The new workspace opens on the home screen with the setup checklist. Your trial starts at once. Connect a support email address next so customer messages start arriving as tickets.
