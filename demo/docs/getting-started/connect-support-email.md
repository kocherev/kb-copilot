# Connect your support email

Customer emails become tickets when you forward your support address to Northstar Desk.

## Add the address

Open Settings > Channels > Email and choose Add address. Enter the address customers already write to, such as help@yourcompany.com. Northstar Desk shows a forwarding address in the form slug@inbound.northstardesk.example.

## Set up forwarding

In your email provider, create a rule that forwards every message sent to your support address to the forwarding address. Northstar Desk then sends a verification code to your support address. Because forwarding is on, the code arrives as a ticket in your workspace. Copy it into the setup screen to finish.

## How many addresses you can connect

The Starter plan includes 1 support address. The Team plan includes up to 10 addresses, and Enterprise has no limit. Each address can have its own signature and its own default group.

## Test it

Send an email from a personal account to the support address. It should appear as a new ticket within a minute. Reply from Northstar Desk and check that the reply reaches the personal inbox. If the reply lands in spam, verify your sending domain as described on the email channel page.
