# Work your first ticket

A ticket is one conversation with a customer. This page explains statuses and priorities so your team uses them the same way.

## Ticket statuses

Every ticket has one of six statuses: New, Open, Pending, On hold, Solved, or Closed. New means nobody has looked at it yet. Pending means you are waiting for the customer to answer. On hold means you are waiting for someone inside your company or a third party. Solved means the agent believes the issue is fixed.

## Automatic closing

A solved ticket closes automatically after 4 days without a customer reply. On the Team and Enterprise plans an Admin can set this window anywhere from 1 to 28 days in Settings > Tickets. A closed ticket cannot be reopened. If the customer writes again, Northstar Desk creates a follow-up ticket linked to the closed one.

## Priorities

Priorities are Low, Normal, High, and Urgent. New tickets start as Normal unless a rule changes them. Urgent tickets can trigger a Slack notification when the Slack integration is connected.

## Replies and internal notes

A public reply is emailed to the customer. An internal note is visible only to your team. Light agents can add internal notes but cannot send public replies.
