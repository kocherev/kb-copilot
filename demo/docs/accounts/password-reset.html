Reset your password

Use these steps when you forget your Northstar Desk password.

Request a reset link

On the sign-in page, choose Forgot password and enter your work email address. We send a reset link to that address.

Password reset links expire after 30 minutes. If the link has expired, request a new one. Only the newest link works.

Password rules

  • A password must be at least 12 characters long.
  • You cannot reuse any of your last 5 passwords.
  • Changing your password signs you out on every other device.

Locked sign-in

After 5 failed sign-in attempts in a row, sign-in for that account is locked for 15 minutes. Waiting is the only way to unlock it early. Support cannot remove the lock.

Workspaces with single sign-on

If your workspace enforces SSO, password reset is turned off for everyone except the Owner. Reset your password with your identity provider instead.
