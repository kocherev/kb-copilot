# Invite teammates

Admins and the Owner can invite people to the workspace.

## Send invitations

Go to Settings > Team and choose Invite people. Enter up to 50 email addresses at once and pick a role for the group. Each person gets an email with a link to join.

## When invitations expire

An invitation link expires after 7 days. You can resend an invitation from the Pending tab, which sends a new link and starts a new 7-day period. You can also cancel a pending invitation from the same tab.

## Seats

Inviting someone as an Agent or Admin needs a free paid seat. If every seat is taken, the Owner is asked to add a seat before the invitation is sent. Light agent invitations never need a paid seat. The seats page explains how added seats are charged.

## Workspaces that use single sign-on

When SSO is enforced, invited people sign in through your identity provider instead of creating a password. With just-in-time provisioning turned on, people assigned to the Northstar Desk app in your identity provider can join without an invitation at all.

## Removing someone

To remove a person, open their profile in Settings > Team and choose Remove from workspace. Their tickets stay assigned until you reassign them. Removing an Agent frees their seat for someone else. The seat itself stays on your bill until you lower the seat count, which takes effect at the next renewal.
