# Transfer ownership

Each workspace has one Owner. The current Owner can hand that role to someone else, for example when they leave the company.

## Who can receive ownership

The new Owner must already be an Admin in the workspace and must have two-factor authentication turned on. If the person you want is an Agent, make them an Admin first.

## Steps

1. The current Owner opens Settings > Workspace and chooses Transfer ownership.
2. They pick the Admin who will become the new Owner and confirm with their password.
3. The chosen Admin receives an email and must accept within 24 hours.

If the email is not accepted within 24 hours, the request is cancelled and nothing changes.

## What changes

After the transfer, the previous Owner becomes an Admin. Billing emails and invoices go to the new Owner from the next invoice onward. The billing permission that the old Owner gave to other Admins stays in place.

## When the Owner has left

If the Owner has already left the company and cannot sign in, an Admin can contact Northstar support. Support asks for written confirmation from someone authorized at your company and may take up to 5 business days to complete the change.
