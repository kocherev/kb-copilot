# Invoices

Northstar Desk issues an invoice for every charge, including renewals and seats added in the middle of a term.

## Finding your invoices

Invoices stay available for 24 months in the billing tab, under Settings > Billing > Invoices. Each one can be downloaded as a PDF. By default only the Owner sees this page, and Admins with the billing permission see it too.

## Invoice emails

Each invoice is emailed to the billing contact on the day it is issued. The billing contact can be a shared address such as accounts@yourcompany.com, and it does not need a Northstar Desk account. Change it under Settings > Billing > Billing details.

## Invoice numbers

Invoice numbers follow the format NSD-YYYY-NNNNNN, for example NSD-2026-004211. Quote the invoice number when you contact the billing team about a charge.

## Changing company details

Your company name, address, and tax ID come from Billing details. Changes apply to future invoices only. Issued invoices cannot be edited, but the billing team can reissue up to your last 3 invoices with corrected details. Email billing@northstardesk.example with the invoice numbers.

## Invoice currency

An invoice is in the currency your subscription uses: US dollars, euros, or British pounds. The currency is set when you first subscribe and cannot be changed later.
