# Cancel your subscription

Only the Owner can cancel a Northstar Desk subscription.

## Cancel

Open Settings > Billing and choose Cancel subscription. You are asked for a short reason and then to confirm. The subscription will not renew, and your workspace stays fully active until the end of the period you already paid for.

## After the paid period ends

When the paid period ends, the workspace closes and nobody can sign in. Your data is kept for 30 days after that. During those 30 days the Owner can reactivate the subscription and everything comes back exactly as it was.

## Permanent deletion

After the 30 days, the workspace and all its tickets, contacts, and attachments are permanently deleted. Deleted data cannot be recovered by support. If you want a copy, run a data export before you cancel.

## Refunds when you cancel

Cancelling does not create a refund on its own. Annual plans cancelled within 30 days of purchase or renewal can still ask for a pro-rated refund, as the refunds page explains.
