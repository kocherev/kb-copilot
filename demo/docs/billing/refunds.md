# Refunds

This page explains which charges can be refunded and how long a refund takes.

## Who can get a refund

Annual plans can get a pro-rated refund for the unused full months, if you ask within 30 days of the purchase or renewal date. Monthly plans are not refunded. You can cancel a monthly plan at any time, and it stays active until the end of the month you paid for.

## Refund timing

The billing review team answers every refund request within 2 business days. Refunds take 5 business days after the review team approves the request. The money goes back to the original payment method. If that card has expired, your bank usually passes the refund to your new card.

## How to request a refund

The Owner can request a refund from Settings > Billing > Request refund. You can also email billing@northstardesk.example from the billing contact address. Include the invoice number and the reason for the request.

## What is not refunded

Seats you remove in the middle of a term are not refunded. They stay on your plan until the next renewal. Service credits under the Enterprise uptime commitment are applied to a future invoice and are never paid out as cash. Refunds are paid in the currency of the original invoice.
