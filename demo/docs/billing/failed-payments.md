# Failed payments

Sometimes a renewal charge fails, for example when a card expires. This page explains what happens next.

## Retries

A failed renewal retries twice, then the workspace moves to a read-only plan. The first retry happens 3 days after the failed charge and the second retry happens 7 days after it. The billing contact gets an email after each failed attempt.

## Read-only mode

In read-only mode your team can still sign in and read tickets, but nobody can send replies, and new customer emails are held instead of becoming tickets. Held emails are delivered as tickets once the workspace is active again. Integrations and the API stop sending changes while the workspace is read-only.

## Getting back to normal

Update the payment method under Settings > Billing > Payment method. The unpaid invoice is retried at once, and the workspace becomes active again as soon as the charge succeeds.

## If nothing changes

If a workspace stays read-only for 60 days, it is scheduled for deletion. The Owner gets warning emails 14 days and 3 days before deletion. Export your data before then if you do not plan to pay.
