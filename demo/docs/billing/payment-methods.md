# Payment methods

The Owner, and Admins with the billing permission, can manage how Northstar Desk is paid.

## Cards we accept

We accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. Prepaid cards are not accepted. Card payments show on the statement as Northstar Desk.

## Bank transfer

Bank transfer is available only for Enterprise plans billed annually. Those invoices have net 30 payment terms. Bank details are printed on each Enterprise invoice.

## Currency

You can pay in US dollars, euros, or British pounds. Pick the currency when you first subscribe. It cannot be changed after the first invoice.

## Update your card

Open Settings > Billing > Payment method and choose Replace card. The new card is used for the next charge. If the workspace is read-only because a payment failed, adding a working card retries the unpaid invoice right away.

## Card security

Card numbers are handled by our payment processor and never stored on Northstar Desk servers. We only keep the card brand, the last four digits, and the expiry date, so you can tell which card is on file.
