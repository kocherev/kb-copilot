# Taxes and VAT

Northstar Desk adds sales tax or VAT to invoices where the law requires it.

## Add your VAT ID

Businesses in the European Union and the United Kingdom can add a VAT ID under Settings > Billing > Tax details. We check the number when you save it. With a valid EU VAT ID, invoices use the reverse charge rule and do not include VAT.

## Sales tax in the United States

For customers in the United States, sales tax is added in the states where software subscriptions are taxable. The tax is based on the billing address in Billing details.

## Tax-exempt organizations

Nonprofits and other tax-exempt organizations in the United States can upload an exemption certificate under Tax details. The billing team reviews certificates within 3 business days. Approved exemptions apply from the next invoice.

## Changing tax details

Changes to your VAT ID, billing address, or exemption status apply to future invoices only. Issued invoices are not recalculated. If an earlier invoice was wrong, ask the billing team to reissue it.
