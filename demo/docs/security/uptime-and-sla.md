# Uptime and service levels

This page explains how we report outages and what the Enterprise uptime commitment covers.

## Status page

Live service status is published at status.northstardesk.example. You can subscribe there to get email or text alerts about incidents and maintenance.

## Scheduled maintenance

We announce scheduled maintenance at least 72 hours in advance on the status page. Scheduled maintenance does not count against the uptime commitment.

## Enterprise uptime commitment

Enterprise plans include a commitment of 99.9% monthly uptime. If uptime in a month falls below 99.9%, you get a service credit of 10% of that month's fee. If it falls below 99.0%, the credit is 25%.

## Claiming a credit

Ask for a credit through support within 30 days after the end of the affected month. Credits are applied to your next invoice. They are not paid out as cash and cannot be refunded.

## Other plans

Starter and Team plans do not include an uptime commitment or service credits. They run on the same infrastructure as Enterprise.
