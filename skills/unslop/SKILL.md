# Plain writing

Edit docs, pull requests, commit messages, and comments before you finish.

- One idea per sentence. Prefer the plain verb. "Use" stays "use".
- Sentence-case headings.
- A colon introduces a list. It does not join two sentences.
- Do not use em dashes.
- Do not bold a label and then repeat it in the same line.
- Cut "additionally", "delve", "enhance", "landscape", "pivotal", "robust", "seamless", "leverage", and "utilize".
- Cut "in order to", "it is important to note", and sentences that could ship in any other project's README.
- Do not force a list into three items.
- Say what the reader can do or measure. "The health route returns 200" is a fact. "A delightful developer experience" is not.
- Straight quotes.
- No emoji in headings.

Run this pass on the README, `AGENTS.md`, and skill prose before they are public. The eval table copies the `make eval` printout. A diagram names the paths that exist.
